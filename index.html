Research Statement

My research integrates ultra-multiplexed system culturomics and systems biology to decode the complex interactions between microbial consortia and host immunity . By engineering multiplexed functional architectures and micro-physiological biointerfaces, I have established a robust framework for modulating the physiological 3D microenvironment and addressing the global challenge of host–microbe interactions and infectious disease modeling.

This technical foundation is supported by recognized leadership and academic excellence, including prestigious fellowships (NIH T32, Kwanjeong, Mogam, KASBP-KAIST). Moving forward, I aim to revolutionize personalized medicine — through an ongoing collaboration with UChicago Medicine on Sepsis–Microbiome Analytical AI Pipelines — by developing an "All-in-One Analytical Platform": a programmable foundry providing cell culture, real-time monitoring and sensing (deep learning 3D segmentation, U-Net), and autonomous perturbation and delivery in 3D for disease modeling, further integrated with multi-omics (deep shotgun sequencing, metabolomics, multi-panel cytokines) through the UChicago DFI team.

Ultimately, my work seeks to harness quantitative AI systems biology to uncover ground biological truth — providing a scalable strategy for rapid clinical intervention in complex host physiological systems and disease modeling for precision medicine.
